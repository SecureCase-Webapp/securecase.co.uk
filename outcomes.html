Outcomes

Prove the place got better.

Issuing notices is an output. A quieter street, a shop that stops calling, a resident who walks through town again: those are outcomes. SecureCase measures them.

Week by week

See demand respond to action.

Incident reporting is broken down by week, area and ASB category, with every figure traceable to the incidents behind it. Filter to a hotspot and the weeks around an intervention, and the effect is visible to anyone in the room.

Figures are operational indicators, clearly labelled, never dressed up as proof of causation.

What gets measured

Outcomes at three levels: person, victim, place.

Place

Hotspot demand and displacement

Weekly incidents by location, premises demand and heat maps for any period, filtered to the streets you care about, so you can see whether a problem is shrinking.

Person

Intervention outcomes

Which interventions were used and when each was escalated, with incident demand at the location before and after, so the ladder is visible rather than assumed.

Victim

Harm and support

High-risk and repeat victims, hate-related cases and open support referrals, with close-out checks before a case can be completed.

Pipeline

Enforcement activity

Letters, CPWs, CPNs, Respect Orders and breaches issued in any window, by case type, with the underlying documents one click away.

Quality

File health

Overdue reviews, missing outcomes, escalations with no linked case, open cases with no officer assigned and back-dated incidents, surfaced before they become a problem in court.

Partnership

Reports you can share

Excel exports and printable views for Community Safety Partnerships and councillors. The summary sheet carries counts only; named detail sits on separate sheets so it can be withheld from wider circulation.

Confidence

Confidence returns when people see follow-through.

The goal is a town centre that people choose to come back to. SecureCase records what was done, when, and what happened next, so authorities can tell that story honestly, and businesses and residents can see that reporting leads to action.

  • Baseline demand captured before an intervention.
  • Incidents compared 90 days before, during and after each programme.
  • Victim contact and updates recorded, not assumed.
  • Each outcome recorded with the officer's assessment of why it was achieved.
