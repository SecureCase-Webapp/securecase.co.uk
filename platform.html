Platform

Everything the method needs, in one workspace.

SecureCase is a secure web application deployed separately for each authority. Officers use it on the desk and on patrol; managers, legal, CCTV and youth justice see the same picture with the access their role requires.

Capabilities

From the street to the courtroom.

People

Person profiles

One record per person with aliases, vulnerability and support notes, youth pathway, status lifecycle and every linked incident, intervention and case.

Places

Incidents, premises and hotspots

Geolocated incidents, premises demand and heat maps show where harm concentrates for any period you choose.

Patrol

Mobile patrol view

Officers log incidents, check known people and attach photographs from the street, with briefings built from live demand.

Victims

Victim register

Risk level, repeat victimisation, hate elements, support referrals and contact history, with a close-out gate so nobody is forgotten when a case ends.

Evidence

Evidence and exhibits

Body-worn and CCTV video, photographs, documents and statements with exhibit labelling, signed-copy tracking and authenticated downloads. Respect Order files add fixed exhibit numbers and tamper-evident hashes.

Early intervention

Warning letters and support

Warning letters generated from the incidents on file, with support offered and the response recorded, so the escalation that follows is evidenced.

Enforcement

CPW, CPN and summons

Community Protection Warnings and Notices produced from your approved templates, with service, signed copies and breach cases linked back to the originals.

Respect Orders

Respect Order case files

The leading power for persistent adult ASB. A dedicated workspace walks the officer from incidents and evidence through early intervention, risk assessment and officer statement to an indexed court file, with fixed exhibit numbers and tamper-evident documents. Breach cases link straight back to the order. Delivered as a gated module, switched on once the power is in force and your legal team has approved the templates.

Court

Statements, breaches and bundles

Officer statements, breach statements, draft orders and indexed court bundles assembled from the evidence already on the case — Word and PDF.

Youth

Youth ASB pathway

A separate, safeguarding-aware flow for under-18s with parent, school and youth justice involvement recorded at every step.

Reporting

Reports and exports

Weekly incident trends, ASB categories, premises demand, intervention outcomes, victim impact and officer workload — on screen and in Excel.

Governance

Audit, retention and access

Role-based access, two-factor authentication, audit trails, retention review, lawful-basis records and subject access exports.

Assistance, controlled

AI that drafts. Officers who decide.

When an authority chooses to enable it, SecureCase can draft statements from the incidents on file, summarise long footage, transcribe audio and check a court file for gaps. Every output is a draft for the officer to review, and nothing is enabled until the data-processing arrangements are agreed.

Providers are configurable per authority, including a UK-processed option.

Deployment

Your authority, your boundary.

Each deployment runs separately with its own database, evidence store and configuration. Hosting is UK-based, and the platform runs on standard, well-supported infrastructure your IT team already understands.

Per authority
Isolated deployment — no shared casework database between councils.
UK hosted
Data stays in the United Kingdom. Evidence lives in a private store and is only ever served through authenticated, role-checked downloads.
Open standards
Word, PDF and Excel outputs; CSV and calendar exports; no proprietary lock-in for your documents.
Tested
Automated regression suites run on every change, covering workflows, documents, security boundaries and rendering.
