Why SecureCase exists

Perception is the problem. Evidence is the answer.

People stop visiting a town centre long before the statistics catch up. Shutters, groups on the corner, street drinking, intimidation outside the shops — each one small, together decisive.

Most authorities respond with letters, meetings and good intentions, then lose the thread. SecureCase keeps it: every incident logged against a person and a place, every intervention recorded, every exhibit ready for court.

What we do

A practitioner's method, built into the software.

SecureCase is not generic case management. It replicates the way an experienced ASB officer actually works, so every team can operate to the same standard.

01 · Identify

See the pattern early

Incidents from officers, CCTV, patrols and partners land on one map and one person profile. Repeat harm shows itself within weeks, not years.

02 · Evidence

Build the file as you go

Body-worn video, CCTV, photographs, statements and signed notices attach to the right case and chronology, with signed copies kept under their originals and fixed exhibit numbers on Respect Order files.

03 · Intervene

Offer the way out first

Warning letters, support referrals and follow-up are built in, with safeguarding recorded alongside enforcement so the file shows the help that was offered.

04 · Escalate

Respect Orders, built on evidence

CPWs and CPNs lead to the Respect Order — the new power for persistent adult ASB, where breach is a criminal offence. Each step is evidenced by what came before.

05 · Court-ready

Statements and bundles from the file

Respect Order applications, officer statements and indexed court bundles are produced from the evidence already on the case — not rebuilt the night before.

06 · Measure

Prove the place improved

Weekly incident trends, hotspot maps, victim impact and intervention outcomes show councillors, partners and the public what changed.
