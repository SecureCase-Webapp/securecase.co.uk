Trust & security

Sensitive work deserves serious protection.

ASB casework involves vulnerable people, children, victims and evidence that may end up in court. SecureCase is designed so information-governance, legal and ICT teams can see exactly how it is handled.

Controls

Security built into the way the platform works.

Isolation

One deployment per authority

Separate application, database, evidence store and configuration for each council. No shared casework data between organisations.

Access

Role-based, least privilege

Administrator, manager, officer, police, CCTV and youth justice roles see only what their function needs. Gated modules require explicit case membership.

Identity

Two-factor authentication

Email-based second factor on sign-in, session protection and CSRF defences on every form that changes data.

Evidence

Protected evidence delivery

Uploaded evidence is never served directly. Every download passes through authentication, record lookup and role checks. Respect Order documents carry SHA-256 integrity hashes that are verified on every download.

Accountability

Audit trail

Significant actions are recorded with who, when and what — supporting management review, complaints and legal scrutiny.

Lifecycle

Retention and subject access

Retention review, lawful-basis records and subject access exports are built in, not bolted on.

AI processing

Optional, governed, and always reviewed by an officer.

SecureCase can use AI to draft statements, summarise footage, transcribe audio and check court files. It is enabled only when an authority chooses to configure it and the data-processing arrangements have been agreed.

  • Provider is configurable per authority, including a UK-processed option.
  • Every output is a draft; nothing is filed, served or sent automatically.
  • Prompts and outputs are task-scoped — drafting, summary, review, vision and transcription are governed separately.
  • Documented in the DPIA support pack and sub-processor register supplied with the platform.

What AI does

  • Suggests a first draft from recorded facts.
  • Points out gaps before legal review.
  • Saves hours of transcription and summarising.

What AI never does

  • Decide whether to escalate.
  • Submit anything to a court or partner.
  • Run without the authority's explicit configuration.
